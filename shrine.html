pinned characters / personal favorites

The people I keep coming back to.

Five favorites across both anime installments. Different lives, different kinds of power, and something human beneath the legend. Character impressions below stay clear of ending reveals.

01 / ambition & belonging

David Martinez

A bigger life. A dangerous pace.

David starts as a young outsider and throws himself into the edgerunner life. His drive gives the first anime its momentum, but the human pull is his need to belong. His story turns ambition into a question: how much of yourself can you give to a dream before it stops being yours?

The jacket, the determination, and the heart beneath the chrome.

01 / distance & connection

Lucy

The moon is more than a destination.

Lucy brings a quieter intensity: a skilled netrunner with a guarded past and a dream beyond Night City. Beside David’s forward rush, her desire for escape gives the story a different horizon. Their connection makes that distant moon feel less like scenery and more like the possibility of another life.

A netrunner, a private dream, and a little room to breathe.

02 / chrome & self-definition

Talia

Who gets to decide who you become?

Talia moves between a corporate upbringing and Maelstrom’s obsession with augmentation. That contrast makes her a compelling part of the second anime: someone trying to define herself through a world of extreme reinvention. Her story invites a closer look at the line between choosing change and being consumed by it.

Corpo origins. Maelstrom chrome. An identity under pressure.

02 / corporate power

Kiril

Talia’s father. The corpo side of the story.

Kiril is Talia’s father and a Kang Tao executive. He brings the corporate world into the family story, where authority and personal relationships occupy the same space. As a character, he makes an interesting counterpoint to Talia: institutional power on one side, an urge to break away on the other.

One of my favorites as a character. A very different kind of Night City presence.

02 / the person after the legend

King / Weak Kingsley

What remains when the legend fades?

Once known as King, Weak Kingsley is a veteran edgerunner living beyond his glory days. Where David’s story surges toward possibility, King’s looks back at the cost of a reputation. His presence gives the second anime a different weight: a person trying to find purpose beyond what the city used to call him.

King belongs here for the man behind the name, as much as the legend.
